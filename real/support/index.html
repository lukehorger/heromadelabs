Hero Made Real: Support

Hero Made Real is a habit RPG for adults: your real habits power your hero's fight. Need help? We're here.

Contact us

Email real@heromadelabs.com. We're a small studio, and we reply as soon as we can.

Testing the founding alpha?

  • Send feedback from TestFlight: take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback.
  • If the app crashes, TestFlight asks if you want to send a report. Please say yes.

Delete your account

  • In the app: go to Social, then Account, then "Delete Social account".
  • By email: write to real@heromadelabs.com, and we will delete what we hold about you within 30 days.
  • Your game is kept on your phone. To clear it, start fresh in the game or delete the app.

Privacy and terms

  • Privacy policy: what the app keeps, what leaves your phone, and who gets it. No ads, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.
