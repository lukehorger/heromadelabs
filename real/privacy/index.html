Hero Made Real: Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 November 2026. Last updated: 4 November 2026.

Luke Horger, doing business as Hero Made Labs ("we", "us") makes Hero Made Real, a game for iPhone and Android. Hero Made Labs LLC has been filed in Hawaii. Once the state approves it, the LLC takes over this policy, and we will tell you in the app. This policy says what the app keeps, what leaves your phone, who gets it, and how to have it deleted.

Questions about this policy: real@heromadelabs.com.

1. Your game stays on your phone

  • Your days, habits, defenses, sleep times, notes, XP, items, hero and story are kept on your phone. We do not get them.
  • You can save your game to a file. That file is yours, and it goes only where you put it.
  • The app does not read the words you type, with one exception. When you type a habit's name, your phone checks whether it matches one of that area's habit buttons, ignoring capital letters and spaces at the ends, so the habit counts. This check happens on your phone only.
  • Some pictures are made on your phone and never leave it:
    • the chapter picture the app draws for each letter;
    • the picture of your hero's face from the equipment and look screens.
  • The fight scenes send nothing anywhere.
  • We have no ads of any kind, no analytics, no tracking, and no crash reports that include anything you wrote.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data for ads or marketing.

2. What leaves your phone, and only when you choose it

Nothing in this section leaves your phone unless you do the thing that sends it. There is one exception: once you turn on group boards for a group (2b), the two board numbers are sent again after each day you seal. Sealing a day never sends anything else.

2a. If you make an account (for Social and the Mythic Reckoning)

  • What we get:
    • your email address, to send you a sign-in email;
    • the name you choose to show;
    • the people you connect with and the private groups you join;
    • the things you choose to share with them;
    • anyone you block;
    • any safety report you make.
  • Social is invite-only. You share with people only when you write or pick something, check it, and tap to send it to the people or private group you chose.
  • Your age: when you sign in, you confirm "I am 18 or older." We do not ask for your date of birth, and we keep no record of the tick itself.
  • Who holds it: our Social service, which runs on Supabase.
  • The sign-in email: Resend sends it for us, from send.heromadelabs.com.
    • What Resend gets: your email address, and the email itself, which holds your sign-in link or code. Each stops working after an hour.
    • How it works for us: as a service provider, under our instructions, mainly in the United States.
    • What it keeps: the email and its delivery details while our Resend account is open. It deletes them within 90 days of that account ending.
    • No tracking: we keep Resend's open and click tracking off, so it does not learn when or where you read the email.
  • How long we keep it: until you delete your account (section 4).

2b. Group boards, only if you turn them on for a group

  • What goes: two numbers, to that group only:
    • how far your run has gone (Campaign Trail);
    • how many of the last 7 days hold a sealed day with at least one real habit or kept defense (Steady Flame).
  • With them go the date they were worked out for, and the name you already show in that group.
  • Nothing else goes: no habit, note, label, time, place or anything else you logged.
  • They follow your record: both numbers are worked out fresh from your sealed days, so a corrected day corrects them.
  • Turning boards on and off: they are off until you turn them on for a group. Once on, they are sent again after each day you seal. Turning boards off removes your numbers from our server, and so does deleting your account.

2c. If you send a week to the Mythic Reckoning

  • What it is: a Game Master's letter about your week, written by an AI. The Mythic Reckoning is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • When anything goes: only when you tap Send for that week, after you have seen exactly what will be sent.
  • What goes:
    • the one weekly summary of your week the app makes for the Game Master: your habits, defenses, sleep times and XP for that week, and how your run went;
    • your notes, reflections and the names you gave things in your own words, unless you untick them (you can, for any week, before sending);
    • a look-back: counts only, from up to 12 earlier weeks, and the one idea your last letter offered you to try. It has no notes, reflections or words of your own.
      • The look-back is on until you switch it off.
      • The first time it would go, the app tells you what it is and how to switch it off.
      • You see it with the rest before every Send.
  • Who gets it: our relay, a small program we run on Cloudflare, carries it to Anthropic, whose AI writes your letter.
    • The relay keeps nothing of your week or of the look-back.
    • Anthropic does not use what we send to train its AI, and deletes it within 30 days, unless the law or its safety rules require it to keep it longer.
  • What the relay does keep: only these facts about your account, so it can check your plan and count letters. They are kept in a small Cloudflare database:
    • your account's id, a random code that is not your name or email;
    • whether you have a Mythic plan, and until when;
    • for each of your last 12 weeks, how many letters you received or were paid for (at most 3 a week);
    • how many of your two tries at your one free Reckoning you have used;
    • for each of your last 12 weeks, how many Reckoning pictures you asked for.
    • Nothing else: no part of your week, letter, email, name, device or address.
  • Deleting your account deletes all of these facts (section 4).
  • Your letter comes back to your phone. Until you accept it, it waits there. Accepting it or letting it go ends the wait. The relay keeps no copy.

2d. If you ask the Game Master a question

  • Who can ask: a Mythic player may ask up to 3 questions a week about a week they have already sent.
  • What goes: the question, the week it is about, and the letter you received for that week, to the same AI service (Anthropic), through our relay.
  • When: only when you tap Send for that question, after you have seen exactly what will be sent.
  • What the relay keeps: no question, no letter and no answer.

2e. If you turn on the Reckoning picture

  • What it is: a picture of your week, drawn by an AI, to go with your letter. It is off until you turn it on. Turning it on asks your permission and names the service. You can turn it off at any time.
  • The picture brief: a letter may come with one or two sentences the Game Master writes about your week.
    • By default it leaves out other people's names, exact places and times, health details, money, and anything from your notes.
    • You can change the brief, or skip it.
  • When anything goes: only when you tap to draw it, after you have seen exactly what will be sent.
  • What goes:
    • the brief, as you left it;
    • your hero's look, Class and companion, and the place your run has reached.
    • Our relay adds a fixed line about the game's art style. Nothing else goes: not your letter, habits, notes, account id, email or name.
  • Who gets it: our relay carries it to Google, whose Gemini API draws the picture. We use Google's paid service only.
    • Google does not use what we send, or the picture, to train its AI or improve its products.
    • Google keeps them for a limited time, only to detect and prevent misuse, as its terms say.
    • The relay keeps neither the brief nor the picture.
  • The picture comes back to your phone only. It is kept with that week's letter, and in your saved game file if you make one. Every picture carries Google's SynthID mark, an invisible mark that shows it was made by AI.
  • When the picture is off, or one can't be made, your letter shows its chapter picture, which the app draws on your phone and never sends.

2f. If you buy a Mythic plan (iPhone)

  • Apple takes your payment through the App Store. We never see your card or your Apple account. Apple's privacy policy covers that, and Apple keeps its own purchase records.
  • RevenueCat runs subscriptions for us.
    • What it gets: your purchase history (what you bought, when, whether it renews and whether it is still active), and your account's id, the same random code.
    • What it never gets: your days, notes or anything from your game.
    • How we set it up: with no device identifiers and no diagnostics. It works for us, under our instructions.
  • From RevenueCat, our relay learns only whether your plan is on, and until when. Test purchases give no plan. If you restore a purchase on a new account, the plan moves to that account.
  • A plan we give you, for example as an early tester, is kept by the relay in the same way, and in no other way.
  • On Android: the Android app sells nothing. It never contacts RevenueCat, and Google Play takes no payment for Hero Made Real.

2g. During the founding alpha only: sharing a Reckoning with the developer

  • The tick: while Hero Made Real is in its invited founding alpha, you may tick "Share this Reckoning with the developer" for one week at a time. The tick is off every week until you set it, and you see exactly what will be shared.
  • What is shared: that week as you sent it, and the letter you got back, go to the developer, who may review them with an AI assistant, Anthropic's Claude, with its model training switched off.
  • How long: a shared copy is kept no longer than 60 days, and every copy is deleted when the alpha ends.

4. Deleting your data

  • Your game: it is on your phone. You can start fresh in the game, or delete the app. Your letters and pictures are on your phone too.
  • Your account: delete it in the app, under Social, then Account, then "Delete Social account".
    • Deleting it removes your account, your connections, your shares, your board numbers and the relay's account facts (2c).
    • Some records about safety reports, including report details you gave, may stay after your account is deleted, so that reports can be handled safely.
  • Ask us: email real@heromadelabs.com, and we will delete what we hold about you within 30 days.
  • Others keep their own records:
    • Apple and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records.
    • Resend keeps the sign-in emails it sent until it deletes them, as in 2a.
    • Anthropic and Google keep what was sent to them for the limited times in 2c and 2e.
  • Copies we don't hold: we have no copy of your week, letters, questions or pictures to delete.

5. Adults only

  • For adults: Hero Made Real is for people 18 and older, on iPhone and Android. When you sign in, you confirm that you are.
  • Children: we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has made an account, email us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices

  • The Mythic Reckoning is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Before every send, you can untick your notes, reflections and own words, and you can switch off the look-back.
  • The Reckoning picture is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time. You can change or skip any brief.
  • Group boards send only for the groups you turned them on for.
  • Social sharing happens only when you send something yourself.
  • Do Not Track: we do not track you across apps or websites, so there is nothing for a "Do Not Track" signal to change.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change applies.
