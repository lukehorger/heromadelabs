How it works

You live your week. Your hero lives it too.

1Do your habits

The workout, the reading, the meditation. Whatever you're leveling up in real life.

2Your hero fights

Every habit you keep becomes a strike in your hero's battles.

3Your story grows

Each week, the app can write you a new chapter of the story, with pictures, based on how your real week went.

The test

It's the very first version, so expect rough edges. You play it in your real week, then tell us what's fun, what's confusing and what breaks.
