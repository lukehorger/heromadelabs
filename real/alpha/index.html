Be a founding tester for Hero Made Real

Hero Made Real is a habit RPG for adults. Your real-life habits (training, study, focus, anything you're working on) power your hero's fight. And each week, the app can write you a new chapter of the story, with pictures, based on how your real week went.

The test

  • About 30 founding testers. It's the very first version, so expect rough edges.
  • Aimed at January 2027. We'll email you when your invite is ready.
  • iPhone (through Apple's TestFlight) or Android (a Google Play test). Free while you test, and there will always be a free version.
  • You play it in your real week, then tell us what's fun, what's confusing and what breaks.

Who can join

  • You're 18 or older. Why? Not because of anything mature in the game. The app's story chapters are written with AI, and those AI services and the privacy laws for younger users mean we start with adults.
  • You don't live in Washington or Nevada during the test, because of those states' health-data laws.

What we do with your answers

We use your email only to send your tester invite and news about the test. We don't sell or share it, and there are no ads. Want off the list? Email real@heromadelabs.com and we'll delete it. More in our privacy policy.
