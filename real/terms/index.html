3. The Mythic plan (a subscription)

What it gives:

  • a Game Master's letter about each week you send, written by an AI, with up to 3 letters for each week;

What it never gives:

  • fight power;
  • a loot roll or a better chance at loot;
  • protection for a streak;
  • a pause, a revive, or anything that stops a loss;
  • a companion, or a companion's growth or move;
